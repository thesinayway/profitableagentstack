Why we size stacks by GTM team size, not funding stage

Published 2026-11-05 by Profitable Agent Stack, operated by The Sinay Way LLC

Funding rounds say little about what tools you need. How many people do go-to-market says a lot.

Two companies can raise the same round and have very different go-to-market teams: one founder selling alone, the other with fifteen people. They need different tools and should pay different amounts.

The five sizes

  • Solo founder: 1 GTM person
  • Small GTM team: 2–5
  • Early scaling: 6–15
  • Scaling: 16–40
  • Large / multi-team: 40+

What changes as the team grows

  • Cost shifts from tools to seats. Per-sender and per-seat pricing starts to dominate around the point where several people run LinkedIn outreach.
  • Admin matters. At some size you need roles, permissions and a way to offboard people.
  • Signals earn their place later than you think. See when to skip intent data.
  • Procurement shows up. Large teams need security documents and sometimes a sales call, and we say so when a tool is contact-sales.

How we pick tools for each size

A tool's value score is category-wide and leans on its lowest published price, so a free plan can make a tool look like the best value for every team. For each size we also check who reviews and uses the tool: G2's reviewer company-size shares (50 or fewer employees, 51-1,000, over 1,000), customer stories that name team size, and for large teams security and admin evidence such as SSO, roles and SOC 2. That is why the 40+ stack counts a paid Apollo plan and picks Clay for enrichment, while solo and small stacks stay cost-first. Details are on the methodology page.
