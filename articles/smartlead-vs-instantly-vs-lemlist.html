Smartlead vs Instantly vs lemlist: which outbound tool fits a small GTM team?

by Limor Sinay, Profitable Agent Stack | Artificial Intelligence (AI), Blog Posts

Smartlead, Instantly and lemlist are outbound email sequencers, not full autonomous AI SDRs. For a 2–5 person GTM team, Instantly starts at $47/mo on Growth (email-only, 5,000 emails); Smartlead’s Base plan starts at $39/mo and is built around many mailboxes; lemlist’s Email plan starts at $69/mo monthly ($55 annual) and adds LinkedIn and other channels on Multichannel ($109/mo, or $87 annual). Salesforge (from $48/mo) and Woodpecker are other published-price options in the same use case. List prices last verified 2026-11-23; always verify with the vendor. Not advice.

Direct answer: If you already have a list and want a polished email-only sender, look at Instantly first (Growth $47/mo for 5,000 emails). If you need to run many mailboxes and care most about sending infrastructure, look at Smartlead (affiliate; Base from $39/mo). If you want email plus LinkedIn and other channels in one sequence, look at lemlist (affiliate; Email $69/mo monthly or $55 annual; Multichannel $109/mo or $87 annual). Salesforge (affiliate; Pro $48/mo monthly) and Woodpecker (affiliate; price not yet captured in our file) sit in the same outbound email / sequencing use case when they fit. None of these, on its own, is the same job as an AI SDR.

Prices below are vendor list prices last verified 2026-11-23. Affiliate links (Smartlead, lemlist, Salesforge, Woodpecker) are marked; a commission never changes scores, prices or order. See our disclosure. Instantly has no affiliate link on this site.

What job these tools actually do

All three send and sequence outbound email. They are often compared in the same search as “best AI SDR,” but that label is broader: an AI SDR product is supposed to research, write, send and hand off or book, which we cover separately under AI SDR / agentic SDR. If you are choosing a sequencer for a 2–5 person team, stay on this page. If you want the story of companies that did deploy AI SDRs, see how real companies use AI for GTM.

How to choose, without a fake winner

Choose Instantly when the job is email-only outbound, you already have (or can buy) a list, and you want the lowest published Growth-tier entry among the well-known three ($47/mo for 5,000 emails). It is the recommended outbound pick on our small GTM team stack.

Choose Smartlead when mailbox count and sending infrastructure are the constraint — agencies and teams that connect many inboxes. A Smartlead customer story about Pump.co (vendor-published, updated 3 March 2026) describes nearly 800 accounts and about 130,000 emails in month one, with “close to $200K in pipeline revenue” attributed to those campaigns. That volume is not a default small-team setup; it is evidence the product is used at high send volume. Read the source, and see the AI for GTM roundup for the caveats.

Choose lemlist when you want one sequence that can include LinkedIn and other channels, not only email. The Multichannel plan is the one that adds those channels, at a higher published price. If you prefer a dedicated LinkedIn tool beside a cheaper email sequencer, HeyReach (affiliate) and Waalaxy (affiliate) are in LinkedIn outreach.

Add Salesforge or Woodpecker later, when they fit — for example if a plan, workflow or existing vendor relationship matches your team better than the three above. We do not treat a missing price (Woodpecker) as a reason to dismiss the tool; we mark it unverified and point you to the vendor.

What this does not decide

  • Deliverability depends on your domains, warmup, copy and list quality, not only on the logo you pick. We do not publish a deliverability ranking of these three.
  • Enrichment is a different job. Clay, FullEnrich (affiliate) and Hunter live on the enrichment page.
  • An AI SDR is a different job. Tools such as Artisan and Qualified are contact-sales in our file. SaaStr’s first-party posts on using them are summarized in how real companies use AI for GTM. For most 2–5 person teams, that is an “add later” category.
