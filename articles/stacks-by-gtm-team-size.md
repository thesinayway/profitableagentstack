# Why we size stacks by GTM team size, not funding stage

by [Limor Sinay](https://profitableagentstack.com/advisory), Profitable Agent Stack | [Artificial Intelligence (AI)](https://profitableagentstack.com/articles/category/ai), [Blog Posts](https://profitableagentstack.com/articles/category/blog-posts)

Funding rounds say little about what tools you need. How many people do go-to-market says a lot.

Two companies can raise the same round and have very different go-to-market teams: one founder selling alone, the other with fifteen people. They need different tools and should pay different amounts.

## The five sizes

- **Solo founder:** 1 GTM person
- **Small GTM team:** 2–5
- **Early scaling:** 6–15
- **Scaling:** 16–40
- **Large / multi-team:** 40+

## What changes as the team grows

- **Cost shifts from tools to seats.** Per-sender and per-seat pricing starts to dominate around the point where several people run LinkedIn outreach.
- **Admin matters.** At some size you need roles, permissions and a way to offboard people.
- **Signals earn their place later than you think.** See [when to skip intent data](https://profitableagentstack.com/articles/when-to-skip-intent-data).
- **Procurement shows up.** Large teams need security documents and sometimes a sales call, and we say so when a tool is contact-sales.

## How we pick tools for each size

A tool's value score is category-wide and leans on its lowest published price, so a free plan can make a tool look like the best value for every team. For each size we also check who reviews and uses the tool: G2's reviewer company-size shares (50 or fewer employees, 51-1,000, over 1,000), customer stories that name team size, and for large teams security and admin evidence such as SSO, roles and SOC 2. That is why the 40+ stack counts a paid Apollo plan and picks Clay for enrichment, while solo and small stacks stay cost-first. Details are on the [methodology page](https://profitableagentstack.com/methodology).

Sizes are planning bands, not rules. Estimates are not advice; verify prices with vendors.
