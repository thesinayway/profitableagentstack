When to skip intent data (and what to do first)

Published 2026-11-05 by Profitable Agent Stack, operated by The Sinay Way LLC

Intent and signals tools help you prioritize. If you have nobody worth prioritizing yet, they are an expensive distraction.

Intent and signals tools answer one question: who should I contact first? That is a great question once you have a list, a message and a sequence that sometimes gets replies. Before that, it is a bill.

A simple test

Skip paid intent data until all three are true:

  1. Your hand-built outbound already books meetings. Not once, repeatedly.
  2. You have more good-fit accounts than you can contact this month.
  3. A better order of contact would change what you do on Monday.

If any of these is false, spend the time on your message and your list instead.

What it costs to be early

On the published prices we checked: RB2B has a free plan and a Starter plan at $79/mo, while Common Room's Essential plan is $2,500/mo billed annually for 5 seats. A solo founder's whole recommended stack costs about $41/mo. Paying for intent data first can cost more than the rest of the stack combined.

What to use instead

  • Solo founder: skip signals entirely. Message your warm network and send simple outbound. See the solo founder stack.
  • Small GTM team: the free RB2B plan is enough, and only once meetings are already coming in.
  • Early scaling: a low-cost visitor-identification tool starts to earn its place.
  • Scaling and above: signals belong in the core stack. Pay for them when a real prioritization problem exists.

One more caution

Visitor identification and intent data raise privacy and consent questions that depend on where your buyers live. Check your obligations before you switch anything on.
