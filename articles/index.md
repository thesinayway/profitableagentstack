## Worth reading

Widely shared pieces by other people on getting the same or better GTM results for less. Links open the original; the notes are ours.

- [The 2025 State of B2B GTM Report](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/2025-state-of-b2b-gtm-report) by Kyle Poyar, Growth Unhinged  
  Survey data on where AI actually moved GTM results, so a 2–5 person team can put a small budget behind the plays that work.
- [What GTM teams are doing with ChatGPT](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/chatgpt-for-gtm) by Kyle Poyar, Growth Unhinged  
  Reader-sourced workflows for a general AI assistant you may already pay for. Worth trying before you add another subscription.
- [6 Months of AI SDRs: What’s Worked, How They Brought In $1M+ in 90 Days, and the Real Data Everyone’s Asking For](https://www.saastr.com/6-months-of-ai-sdrs-whats-worked-how-they-brought-in-1m-in-90-days-and-the-real-data-everyones-asking-for/) by Jason Lemkin, SaaStr  
  A candid look at the weekly time and budget AI SDRs take. Useful for judging when a small team is ready to add one.
- [We Deployed 20+ AI Agents and Replaced Our Entire Human SDR Team. Here’s What Actually Works.](https://www.saastr.com/we-deployed-20-ai-agents-and-replaced-our-entire-sdr-team-heres-what-actually-works-video-pod/) by Jason Lemkin, SaaStr  
  The takeaway for a small team: prove the message yourself first, then hand an agent the follow-ups nobody has time for.
- [How we built Clay’s GTM engineering function](https://thegtme.com/p/how-we-built-clays-gtm-engineering) by The GTM Engineer by Clay, The GTM Engineer  
  A GTM team that runs on a handful of well-connected core tools. A good model for keeping your own stack short.
- [Why AI startups are blowing past revenue milestones that old-school SaaS could only fantasize about](https://www.elenaverna.com/p/why-ai-startups-are-blowing-past) by Elena Verna, Elena’s Growth Scoop  
  How small AI-native teams build what they need with AI instead of buying a tool for every job. The same mindset helps a 2–5 person GTM team.
- [What world-class GTM looks like in 2026 (with Jeanne DeWitt Grosser)](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-the-best-gtm-teams-do-differently) by Lenny Rachitsky, Lenny’s Podcast  
  Vercel’s COO on build vs. buy for GTM tools in the AI era and when a GTM engineer is worth it, so you know what to add later.
