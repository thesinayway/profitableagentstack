When to add AEO (AI search visibility) vs classic SEO

Published 2026-11-05 by Profitable Agent Stack, operated by The Sinay Way LLC

AEO tools track whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers mention your brand. Classic search tools come first; add AEO when AI assistants already send you visits.

AEO (answer engine optimization, also called GEO) tools run a set of buyer questions through AI assistants and report whether your brand is mentioned or cited. Classic SEO tools tell you how you rank in search results. They are different jobs, so we list them as separate use cases: Search performance, Content optimization and AEO (AI search visibility).

A simple order

  1. Search performance first. Google Search Console is free and shows which queries bring visitors. Most AI answers still draw on pages that rank, so this data helps both.
  2. Content optimization when you publish weekly. A tool like Surfer helps each page match what searchers want. Before you publish regularly, it is a bill.
  3. AEO when AI search is a channel. Add a tracker once AI assistants already send you visits you can see in your analytics, or your buyers say they ask ChatGPT first.

What it costs

On the published prices we checked: Peec AI starts at $95/mo for 50 prompts on 3 models, Otterly.AI at $29/mo for 15 prompts, and AthenaHQ and Scrunch at $295 and $250/mo. Profound is contact sales. Compare that with the $41/mo for a whole solo stack.

What we recommend by size

  • Solo founder and small team: skip. Warm leads and outbound produce more pipeline, and you have too little AI-search traffic to measure.
  • Early scaling and up: one dedicated tracker. See the early scaling stack.
  • Large teams: check SSO and security terms; several vendors reserve SSO for an enterprise quote.

A caution

These tools measure answers that change from run to run, and none can guarantee a mention. Treat the numbers as a trend, not a score.
