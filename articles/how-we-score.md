# How we score AI GTM tools (and why cost counts)

by [Limor Sinay](https://profitableagentstack.com/advisory), Profitable Agent Stack | [Artificial Intelligence (AI)](https://profitableagentstack.com/articles/category/ai), [Blog Posts](https://profitableagentstack.com/articles/category/blog-posts)

The value score in plain words: what goes in, what does not, and why money never changes rank.

Every tool in the [directory](https://profitableagentstack.com/directory) gets one number: a **value score** from 0 to 100. Here is exactly what is behind it, so you can disagree with it intelligently.

## What goes in

- **Fit (50%).** An editorial 1–5 judgment of how well the tool does its one job, converted to 0–100. It is an opinion, and we say so.
- **Cost (30%).** Inside each use case, the lowest published starting price scores 100 and the highest scores 40, scaled in between. A tool with no public price gets no cost score and therefore no value score.
- **Sentiment (20%).** Reserved for public G2 or Capterra ratings, used only when we can fetch them with a source and a date. Right now we cannot, so fit and cost carry the score (62.5% and 37.5%).

## What stays out

- **Affiliate links and sponsorships.** Payment never changes a score, a badge or an order. See the [affiliate disclosure](https://profitableagentstack.com/disclosure).
- **Prices we could not verify.** Those are shown as "price not yet verified" instead of guessed.

## Where the cost estimates come from

Each stack on the [stacks-by-GTM-size hub](https://profitableagentstack.com/stages/) adds up published monthly list prices for the stated tiers and seats, with the math shown. They are estimates: they exclude taxes, add-ons, overages and annual discounts, and prices change. Always verify with the vendor.

## Scores do not pick the stack alone

The score ranks tools within one use case. For a stack at a given team size we also weigh who reviews the tool at that size and, for large teams, enterprise readiness such as SSO and security documents. When the two disagree, for example a free plan that tops the score but is not what a 40-person team would run, we follow the size evidence and explain why on the stack page.

## The honest limits

A score cannot know your market, your data or your team. Use it to shortlist, then run a trial. The full method lives on the [methodology page](https://profitableagentstack.com/methodology), and if you spot an error, the [corrections page](https://profitableagentstack.com/corrections) is the fastest way to tell us.
