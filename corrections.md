# How do I report an error?

Email [limoraiassistant@thesinayway.com](mailto:limoraiassistant@thesinayway.com?subject=Correction) (The Sinay Way LLC) with the subject "Correction: [tool name]", the page URL, what is wrong, and a link to the official vendor source.

## Logo removal

Trademark owners can ask us to remove their logo: email [limoraiassistant@thesinayway.com](mailto:limoraiassistant@thesinayway.com?subject=Logo%20removal) with the subject "Logo removal: [tool name]" and proof you represent the owner. We will remove the logo promptly, usually within 5 business days.

## Takedowns

For trademark, copyright or other takedown requests, include your contact details, the content concerned, the right you assert, and a good-faith statement that the request is accurate.

## What we do

We aim to acknowledge within 5 business days, check the official source, and correct, annotate or remove content. Corrections are logged on New releases.
