# How do you handle reviews and ratings?

We never fabricate, buy or edit reviews or ratings. We show only public ratings from G2 or Capterra with a source link and date checked, or a link to the reviews.

## Third-party ratings

Ratings belong to their sources and are shown as of the date checked. They may change. G2 and Capterra are trademarks of their owners and do not endorse us.

## Reader feedback

Feedback submitted through "Share your experience" (not live yet) may be moderated. We will not publish it as a rating, and we will remove content that is fake, abusive, defamatory or from someone with an undisclosed conflict of interest. Vendors may not review their own products.

## Incentives

We do not offer incentives for reviews. If that changes, incentivized feedback will be labeled.
