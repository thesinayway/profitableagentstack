# Who runs Profitable Agent Stack?

A cost-first directory of AI go-to-market tools operated by The Sinay Way LLC as part of its advisory services. We list published prices with source links and dates, and mark tools without public pricing as contact sales. We do not invent prices, features or reviews.

## Who this directory is for

Founders and GTM operators who need to choose AI sales and marketing tools without overspending. It is not a paid ranking, and it is not a substitute for vendor docs or your own trial.

## What you can do here

- [Browse every tool we list](https://profitableagentstack.com/directory), with published prices, the date we last checked them, and a value score when we can compute one.
- [See a recommended stack by GTM team size](https://profitableagentstack.com/stages/), from a solo founder to a 40+ person team, with an estimated monthly cost.
- Read short guides, including [how companies use AI for GTM](https://profitableagentstack.com/articles/how-companies-use-ai-for-gtm) and a [comparison of Smartlead, Instantly and lemlist](https://profitableagentstack.com/articles/smartlead-vs-instantly-vs-lemlist).
- [Read how scoring works](https://profitableagentstack.com/methodology) — use-case fit, published cost, and verified G2 ratings when we have them.

## Editorial rules

We list published prices with a source link and a check date. Tools without public pricing are marked contact sales. Some links are affiliates; that never changes scores, prices or order. See the [disclosure](https://profitableagentstack.com/disclosure).

The site is operated by The Sinay Way LLC as part of its advisory services. [Advisory help](https://profitableagentstack.com/advisory) is a separate engagement, with scope and fees only in a signed agreement. Outcomes are not guaranteed.
