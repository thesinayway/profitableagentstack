Privacy Policy

We collect as little data as possible and do not sell it. The Site has no accounts, no tracking cookies and no working forms. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for cookieless aggregate usage and performance metrics.

Who we are

The Site is operated by The Sinay Way LLC as part of its advisory services.

What we collect

The Site itself does not collect personal data through forms: its feedback form is a placeholder that sends and stores nothing. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use it only to reply and to handle corrections or requests. Hosting providers may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type). Cloudflare Web Analytics collects only aggregate, cookieless usage and performance metrics and does not identify individual visitors; metrics from visitors in the European Union are excluded.

Cookies and analytics

No tracking cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics (automatic injection on this hostname) for cookieless aggregate page-view and performance metrics. It does not set cookies, does not identify visitors, and excludes EU visitors. Google Fonts is loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data and do not use it for advertising. Aggregate analytics are processed by Cloudflare as our analytics provider. We may disclose data if required by law. If we add a form or any tool that collects personal data, we will update this policy first and name the provider.
