**Direct answer:** If you already have a list and want a polished email-only sender, look at [Instantly](/use-cases/outbound-email.html#instantly) first (Growth $47/mo for 5,000 emails). If you need to run many mailboxes and care most about sending infrastructure, look at [Smartlead](/go/smartlead) (affiliate; Base from $39/mo). If you want email plus LinkedIn and other channels in one sequence, look at [lemlist](/go/lemlist) (affiliate; Email $69/mo monthly or $55 annual; Multichannel $109/mo or $87 annual). [Salesforge](/go/salesforge) (affiliate; Pro $48/mo monthly) and [Woodpecker](/go/woodpecker) (affiliate; price not yet captured in our file) sit in the same [outbound email / sequencing](/use-cases/outbound-email.html) use case when they fit. None of these, on its own, is the same job as an [AI SDR](/use-cases/ai-sdr.html).

Prices below are vendor list prices last verified 2026-11-23. Affiliate links (Smartlead, lemlist, Salesforge, Woodpecker) are marked; a commission never changes scores, prices or order. See our [disclosure](/disclosure.html). Instantly has no affiliate link on this site.

## What job these tools actually do

All three send and sequence outbound email. They are often compared in the same search as “best AI SDR,” but that label is broader: an AI SDR product is supposed to research, write, send and hand off or book, which we cover separately under [AI SDR / agentic SDR](/use-cases/ai-sdr.html). If you are choosing a sequencer for a 2–5 person team, stay on this page. If you want the story of companies that *did* deploy AI SDRs, see [how real companies use AI for GTM](/articles/how-companies-use-ai-for-gtm.html).

## How to choose, without a fake winner

**Choose Instantly** when the job is email-only outbound, you already have (or can buy) a list, and you want the lowest published Growth-tier entry among the well-known three ($47/mo for 5,000 emails). It is the recommended outbound pick on our [small GTM team](/stages/small-gtm-team.html) stack.

**Choose Smartlead** when mailbox count and sending infrastructure are the constraint — agencies and teams that connect many inboxes. A [Smartlead customer story about Pump.co](https://www.smartlead.ai/case-study/case-study-claire-hardesty) (vendor-published, updated 3 March 2026) describes nearly 800 accounts and about 130,000 emails in month one, with “close to $200K in pipeline revenue” attributed to those campaigns. That volume is not a default small-team setup; it is evidence the product is used at high send volume. Read the source, and see the [AI for GTM roundup](/articles/how-companies-use-ai-for-gtm.html) for the caveats.

**Choose lemlist** when you want one sequence that can include LinkedIn and other channels, not only email. The Multichannel plan is the one that adds those channels, at a higher published price. If you prefer a dedicated LinkedIn tool beside a cheaper email sequencer, [HeyReach](/go/heyreach) (affiliate) and [Waalaxy](/go/waalaxy) (affiliate) are in [LinkedIn outreach](/use-cases/linkedin.html).

**Add Salesforge or Woodpecker later, when they fit** — for example if a plan, workflow or existing vendor relationship matches your team better than the three above. We do not treat a missing price (Woodpecker) as a reason to dismiss the tool; we mark it unverified and point you to the vendor.

## What this does not decide

- **Deliverability** depends on your domains, warmup, copy and list quality, not only on the logo you pick. We do not publish a deliverability ranking of these three.
- **Enrichment is a different job.** [Clay](/use-cases/enrichment.html#clay), [FullEnrich](/go/fullenrich) (affiliate) and [Hunter](/use-cases/enrichment.html#hunter) live on the [enrichment](/use-cases/enrichment.html) page.
- **An AI SDR is a different job.** Tools such as [Artisan](/use-cases/ai-sdr.html#artisan) and [Qualified](/use-cases/ai-sdr.html#qualified) are contact-sales in our file. SaaStr’s first-party posts on using them are summarized in [how real companies use AI for GTM](/articles/how-companies-use-ai-for-gtm.html). For most 2–5 person teams, that is an “add later” category.
