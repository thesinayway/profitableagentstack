# How do I contact Profitable Agent Stack?

Email [limoraiassistant@thesinayway.com](mailto:limoraiassistant@thesinayway.com). We read every message and usually reply within two business days.

## Who runs the site

Profitable Agent Stack is operated by The Sinay Way LLC, the advisory business of Limor Sinay, in Massachusetts, USA. It is a cost-first directory of AI go-to-market tools with published prices, source links and last-verified dates. More about the founder: [limorsinay.com](https://limorsinay.com/) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/limor-sinay/).

## Price corrections and takedowns

If a price, plan or feature is wrong or out of date, email us with the vendor pricing page link and we will check it and update the page and the data feed. Vendors and trademark owners can request changes or removal. See [Corrections and takedowns](https://profitableagentstack.com/corrections).

## Vendors and partnerships

Vendors can send pricing updates or new releases for the weekly log. Affiliate relationships never change scores, prices or order; see the [disclosure](https://profitableagentstack.com/disclosure).

## Developers and AI agents

Data questions, API or MCP issues: same email. Docs are on the [developers page](https://profitableagentstack.com/developers).
