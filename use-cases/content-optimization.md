## When a content-optimization tool is worth it

These tools help you brief, draft or revise pages so they have a better chance of ranking or being cited. They do not replace a clear offer or a page that answers the question a buyer is actually asking. A 2–5 person GTM team can often start with a search-performance tool they already pay for, a style guide, and reviews from customers, then add a dedicated optimizer when the writing volume justifies another seat.

We currently list tools with published prices below. Compare them against [search performance](https://profitableagentstack.com/use-cases/search-performance) and [answer engine optimization (AEO)](https://profitableagentstack.com/use-cases/aeo) so you do not pay twice for the same job.
