## Positioning work before you buy a tool

This use case is for message and offer research: who you sell to, why they buy, and the words that sound like them. We have not yet listed a dedicated positioning tool with a published price, so this page is a buying guide rather than a ranked list. For most seed and Series A teams, a shared spreadsheet, recorded customer calls and win/loss notes are enough.

Consider a paid research workspace later if interviews are piling up unanalyzed, several people need to share the same evidence, or you are preparing a category or pricing change. Until then, put the time into conversations, not software.
