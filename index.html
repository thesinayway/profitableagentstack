FAQ

What is Profitable Agent Stack?

A cost-first directory that ranks AI go-to-market tools by use case, published price and user sentiment, with estimated monthly stack costs by company stage.

Where do the prices come from?

From each vendor's own pricing page, with a source link and the date checked (2026-10-26). Tools with no public price are marked contact sales.

Do you publish user ratings?

Only real public ratings from G2 or Capterra with a source link and date. Where they cannot be fetched reliably, we link to the reviews instead. We never create reviews or ratings ourselves.
