From the articles

When to skip intent data (and what to do first)

Intent and signals tools help you prioritize. If you have nobody worth prioritizing yet, they are an expensive distraction.

When to add AEO (AI search visibility) vs classic SEO

AEO tools track whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers mention your brand. Classic search tools come first; add AEO when AI assistants already send you visits.

Why we size stacks by GTM team size, not funding stage

Funding rounds say little about what tools you need. How many people do go-to-market says a lot.

FAQ

What is Profitable Agent Stack?

A value-first directory that ranks AI go-to-market tools by use case, real user reviews and published price, with estimated monthly stack costs by GTM team size.

How are the stacks grouped?

By how many people do go-to-market: solo founder (1), small GTM team (2–5), early scaling (6–15), scaling (16–40) and large / multi-team (40+).

Where do the prices come from?

From each vendor's own pricing page, with a source link and the date checked (2026-11-02). Tools with no public price are marked contact sales.

Do you publish user ratings?

Only real public ratings from G2 or Capterra with a source link and date. Where they cannot be fetched reliably, we link to the reviews instead. We never create reviews or ratings ourselves.
