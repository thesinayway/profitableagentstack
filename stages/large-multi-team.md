### Spreadsheet or CRM?

At 40+ the CRM is the system of record; seat counts and admin rules matter more than the list price.

Signals that it is time for a CRM (our rules of thumb, not benchmarks):

- **A second person works the pipeline.** A cofounder, first seller, contractor SDR or agency. Shared sheets struggle with ownership, duplicates and overwritten notes.
- **More than about 20–30 live deals or conversations at once.** Or a follow-up has slipped more than once.
- **You copy sequencer replies into the sheet by hand.** More than about 30 minutes a week. Most CRMs here receive replies from Instantly, Smartlead or lemlist directly.
- **Email and LinkedIn reach the same people.** One record per person stops you double-touching prospects.
- **Investor or board pipeline reporting takes more than about an hour a month.** Stage history and pipeline reports are built into every CRM listed, including free plans (depth varies).
- **Inbound leads arrive or your first sales hire starts.** Forms, routing and activity history make the handoff work.

Starter sheet columns: Company, Contact, Email, LinkedIn URL, Source, Stage, Next step, Next-step date, Deal value, Last touch, Notes. Every CRM listed imports a CSV, so nothing is lost when you switch. List prices as of 2026-11-10 from vendor pricing pages; verify with the vendor. Seat counts follow this page's assumptions. ## FAQ

### How much does an AI GTM stack cost for 40+ GTM people (large / multi-team)?

About at least $7,587/mo (list-price floor) for our recommended stack, estimated from published list prices as of 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-23 (per tool). Assumptions: 40+ GTM people across teams, 25 of them prospecting in Apollo (Organization, billed annually, 3-seat minimum), ~25 LinkedIn senders (HeyReach Agency tier), monthly list prices except Apollo and Common Room (billed annually, Common Room 5 seats). Clay is its Growth monthly list price; its Enterprise tier is custom. Salesloft (outbound) has no public price and counts as $0 here; expect a per-seat, annual quote with a seat minimum. Peec AI is Advanced's monthly list price; SSO needs its Enterprise quote. Enterprise tiers, SSO and security documents are quote-based. This total is a floor, not a quote. Actual cost depends on your usage, billing period, taxes and add-ons.

### What is included in the recommended stack?

Apollo (List building & lead data), Clay (Enrichment & verification), Salesloft (Outbound email / sequencing), HeyReach (LinkedIn outreach), Common Room (Signals, intent & analytics), Google Search Console (Search performance), Surfer (Content optimization), Peec AI (AEO (AI search visibility)), Postiz (Social scheduling), Webflow (Landing pages & websites), Google Analytics (Web analytics), PostHog (Product & funnel analytics), Artisan (AI SDR / agentic SDR).
