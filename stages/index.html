FAQ

How should I pick a size?

Count the people who do go-to-market work (selling, outbound, content, RevOps), not total headcount. If you are between two sizes, start with the smaller stack and add tools when a job becomes a real bottleneck.

Why GTM team size instead of funding stage?

Funding rounds say little about the tools you need; the number of people running go-to-market says a lot, because seats, admin and volume drive both cost and fit.
