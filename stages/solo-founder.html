Spreadsheet or CRM?

A sheet is usually enough while you are the only person selling, live deals stay under about 20 and most come from your network. Move to a free CRM the week you turn on a sequencer, or once a follow-up slips.

Signals that it is time for a CRM (our rules of thumb, not benchmarks):

  • A second person works the pipeline. A cofounder, first seller, contractor SDR or agency. Shared sheets struggle with ownership, duplicates and overwritten notes.
  • More than about 20–30 live deals or conversations at once. Or a follow-up has slipped more than once.
  • You copy sequencer replies into the sheet by hand. More than about 30 minutes a week. Most CRMs here receive replies from Instantly, Smartlead or lemlist directly.
  • Email and LinkedIn reach the same people. One record per person stops you double-touching prospects.
  • Investor or board pipeline reporting takes more than about an hour a month. Stage history and pipeline reports are built into every CRM listed, including free plans (depth varies).
  • Inbound leads arrive or your first sales hire starts. Forms, routing and activity history make the handoff work.

Starter sheet columns: Company, Contact, Email, LinkedIn URL, Source, Stage, Next step, Next-step date, Deal value, Last touch, Notes. Use cases that matter at this size

  • List building & lead data
  • Enrichment & verification
  • Outbound email / sequencing
  • LinkedIn outreach
  • Landing pages & websites
  • Newsletters
  • Web analytics

Skip for now

  • Signals & intent data (RB2B, PostHog, Common Room). Paid intent and visitor-identification tools tell you who to talk to. Until hand-built outbound gets replies, you have nobody to prioritize.
  • AI SDR / agentic SDR. These are contact-sales products. An agent cannot fix messaging that does not work by hand yet.
  • Social scheduling, search and content tools, design/video, community. Useful later; none of them is needed to get the first customers. Free Search Console is worth setting up when you publish.
  • AEO (AI search visibility) tools. No AI-search demand to measure yet, and warm leads and outbound come first. Revisit when content gets traffic.
