Small GTM team: AI GTM stack for 2–5 GTM people, about $106/mo

About $106/mo + €38 Waalaxy (2 users) for our recommended stack, estimated from published list prices as of 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-05 (per tool). Assumptions: 3 GTM people, 2 LinkedIn users, ~5,000 emails/mo, monthly billing, entry tiers. Waalaxy is priced per user in euros and not converted. Actual cost depends on your usage, billing period, taxes and add-ons.

Who this is for

Two to five people share go-to-market. Warm intros and hand outbound already produce some meetings, and you now want a repeatable sequence plus a little content.

Recommended stack (estimate)

One tool per job, no paid overlap. Each pick weighs two things: its value score and whether it fits a team of this size, judged from reviews by companies of similar size and from enterprise-readiness evidence. The value score is a category-wide number based on each tool's lowest published price (often a free plan), so a tool can be "best in category" and still not be the pick at this size. "Best in category" means the highest value score (ties included) among all tools we list for that use case.
