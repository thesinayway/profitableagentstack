# Scaling: AI GTM stack for 16–40 GTM people, about $2,193/mo

About $2,193/mo for our recommended stack, estimated from published list prices as of 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-23 (per tool). Assumptions: 25 GTM people, 10 LinkedIn senders, high email volume (125,000/mo), monthly list prices except Apollo (10 prospecting seats, billed annually); Webflow and Surfer are yearly-billed views. RB2B Pro is a 'from' price. PostHog counted at its free allowance (usage-based beyond it). Peec AI is its Pro monthly list price. Actual cost depends on your usage, billing period, taxes and add-ons.

## Who this is for

Sixteen to forty people in go-to-market, several managers, and RevOps starting to own reports, permissions and data hygiene.

### Spreadsheet or CRM?

At this size the CRM is the system of record; a spreadsheet works only for side lists.

Signals that it is time for a CRM (our rules of thumb, not benchmarks):

- **A second person works the pipeline.** A cofounder, first seller, contractor SDR or agency. Shared sheets struggle with ownership, duplicates and overwritten notes.
- **More than about 20–30 live deals or conversations at once.** Or a follow-up has slipped more than once.
- **You copy sequencer replies into the sheet by hand.** More than about 30 minutes a week. Most CRMs here receive replies from Instantly, Smartlead or lemlist directly.
- **Email and LinkedIn reach the same people.** One record per person stops you double-touching prospects.
- **Investor or board pipeline reporting takes more than about an hour a month.** Stage history and pipeline reports are built into every CRM listed, including free plans (depth varies).
- **Inbound leads arrive or your first sales hire starts.** Forms, routing and activity history make the handoff work.

Starter sheet columns: Company, Contact, Email, LinkedIn URL, Source, Stage, Next step, Next-step date, Deal value, Last touch, Notes. Every CRM listed imports a CSV, so nothing is lost when you switch. List prices as of 2026-11-10 from vendor pricing pages; verify with the vendor. Seat counts follow this page's assumptions.
