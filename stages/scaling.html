Scaling: AI GTM stack for 16–40 GTM people, about $1,948/mo

About $1,948/mo for our recommended stack, estimated from published list prices as of 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-05 (per tool). Assumptions: 25 GTM people, 10 LinkedIn senders, high email volume (125,000/mo), monthly list prices except Apollo (10 prospecting seats, billed annually); Webflow and Surfer are yearly-billed views. RB2B Pro is a 'from' price. PostHog counted at its free allowance (usage-based beyond it). Actual cost depends on your usage, billing period, taxes and add-ons.

Who this is for

Sixteen to forty people in go-to-market, several managers, and RevOps starting to own reports, permissions and data hygiene.

Recommended stack (estimate)

One tool per job, no paid overlap. Each pick weighs two things: its value score and whether it fits a team of this size, judged from reviews by companies of similar size and from enterprise-readiness evidence. The value score is a category-wide number based on each tool's lowest published price (often a free plan), so a tool can be "best in category" and still not be the pick at this size. "Best in category" means the highest value score (ties included) among all tools we list for that use case.

  • Warm network & referrals. Sourced and tracked in the CRM, not a tool in this stack.
  • ICP & positioning. Owned by product marketing; no paid tool listed.
