Large / multi-team: AI GTM stack for 40+ GTM people, at least $7,587/mo

About at least $7,587/mo (list-price floor) for our recommended stack, estimated from published list prices as of 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-09 (per tool). Assumptions: 40+ GTM people across teams, 25 of them prospecting in Apollo (Organization, billed annually, 3-seat minimum), ~25 LinkedIn senders (HeyReach Agency tier), monthly list prices except Apollo and Common Room (billed annually, Common Room 5 seats). Clay is its Growth monthly list price; its Enterprise tier is custom. Salesloft (outbound) has no public price and counts as $0 here; expect a per-seat, annual quote with a seat minimum. Peec AI is Advanced's monthly list price; SSO needs its Enterprise quote. Enterprise tiers, SSO and security documents are quote-based. This total is a floor, not a quote. Actual cost depends on your usage, billing period, taxes and add-ons.

Who this is for

More than forty people across several teams or regions. Procurement, security review and data-processing agreements are part of buying anything.

Recommended stack (estimate)

One tool per job, no paid overlap. Each pick weighs two things: its value score and whether it fits a team of this size, judged from reviews by companies of similar size and from enterprise-readiness evidence. The value score is a category-wide number based on each tool's lowest published price (often a free plan), so a tool can be "best in category" and still not be the pick at this size. "Best in category" means the highest value score (ties included) among all tools we list for that use case.

Vendor enterprise page cites RBAC, permission profiles and audit logging; vendor customer story: Superside runs 130 reps on Apollo. These are vendor statements. Source
  • Apollo (G2 reviewer size). G2 comparison page (checked 2026-11-05): of 9,858 Apollo reviews, 67.0% small-business (50 or fewer employees), 28.4% mid-market (51-1,000) and 4.6% enterprise (over 1,000). That is roughly 2,800 mid-market and 450 enterprise reviewers (our arithmetic from G2's percentages). Source
  • ZoomInfo (not in our stack: contact sales). G2 reviewers (8,728 reviews, checked 2026-11-05) are 25.8% small-business, 48.1% mid-market and 26.1% enterprise, the most enterprise-heavy list-building voice we found. It publishes no price (priced by licenses and credits). Vendor security materials state SOC 2 Type II and ISO certifications. Request a quote next to Apollo's. Source
  • Clay (enrichment). Vendor pricing page (checked 2026-11-05): states SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR and CCPA. Enterprise (custom pricing, annual commitment) adds SSO/SAML, SCIM, RBAC, user groups with credit budgets, IP allowlists, and a custom CSA and DPA; Launch and Growth do not include SSO or RBAC. Named customer case studies on the page include Intercom, Verkada, Rippling and Hex. Growth is $495/mo monthly list ($446 annual view). G2: 234 reviews, 73.8% small-business, 24.8% mid-market, 1.4% enterprise; Hunter (659 reviews) shows 31.9% mid-market. Source
  • Salesloft (outbound, our Large pick). G2 comparison page (checked 2026-11-05): 4,287 reviews, 16.5% small-business, 50.9% mid-market, 32.6% enterprise; G2 ease of setup 8.6 vs Outreach 7.7, quality of support 8.6 vs 8.3. Vendor security page lists SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001; its security measures document also lists ISO 27701; help center documents SAML SSO (Okta, OneLogin, Google, Microsoft Entra). Trust center is public. Pricing page goes to a talk-to-sales form (no list price). Vendor says Salesloft is now the company and platform brand after merging with Clari. Source
  • Salesloft (security and SSO). Security & compliance page and help center (checked 2026-11-05). Source
  • Outreach (outbound, also consider). G2 comparison page (checked 2026-11-05): 3,574 reviews, 19.1% small-business, 59.2% mid-market, 21.7% enterprise; reviewers flag cost and a steeper learning curve more often. Vendor trust page lists SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, ISO 42001 and HIPAA, but trust center access is through an account exec. Pricing page lists Amplify Essentials, Core, Plus and Pro with 10,000 to 100,000 AI credits and says pricing is per user plus consumption; no list price. Source
  • Reply.io and HeyReach spot-check. Reply.io, our previous Large outbound pick, is 80.5% small-business, 17.4% mid-market, 2.1% enterprise (1,568 G2 reviews), so it moved to the all-tools list as a cheaper option. HeyReach (LinkedIn) has only 74 G2 reviews, so the LinkedIn pick is unchanged and no listed LinkedIn tool has a large enterprise review base. Source
  • Common Room. Vendor security page lists a SOC 2 Type 2 report, GDPR and CCPA compliance, SSO/SAML/SCIM and role-based access control. Source
