What we found when checking enterprise readiness (as of 2026-11-05)

Statements come from each vendor's own pages or documents linked below. They may change; confirm security, SSO and compliance in writing before buying. This is not legal or compliance advice.

  • Common Room. Vendor security page lists a SOC 2 Type 2 report, GDPR and CCPA compliance, SSO/SAML/SCIM and role-based access control. Source
  • Reply.io. Help center documents organization, multi-team and single-team roles and permissions; vendor white-label page says the platform is SOC 2 certified. Source
  • HeyReach. Help center documents Admin and Member roles with per-feature permissions. Its data processing addendum says SSO (including SAML) is not currently supported, and we found no SOC 2 claim. Source
  • Hunter. Help center: unlimited team members at no extra cost on one shared credit pool, with Owner, Admin and Member roles. Vendor says a DPA is included and SOC 2 is for enterprise. Source
  • Instantly. Public sources we found disagree on whether a SOC 2 Type II report is held. Ask the vendor for its current report under NDA before buying. Source
