What should a pre-seed to Series A team prioritize?

Spend on the use cases that create pipeline first: list building, enrichment and outbound (email + LinkedIn), then signals that tell you who is already interested. Content tools come next. AI SDR platforms are mostly contact-sales and are optional at this stage. Revisit seat counts monthly; per-sender pricing (e.g. LinkedIn tools) is where costs grow fastest as you hire.

What marketing should a pre-seed to Series A team add?

Start with free search data (Google Search Console), one landing page or site with your own domain, and a newsletter on a free plan. Add paid content optimization only once you publish weekly. Social scheduling and video are already covered by Postiz and HeyGen, and analytics by PostHog, so we do not list a second tool for them.

How does cost grow when the team grows from 3 to 10?

In our estimates, LinkedIn senders and signals tools drive most of the increase: HeyReach goes from 2 to 10 senders ($158 to $790/mo), and intent platforms such as Common Room start at $2,500/mo billed annually.
