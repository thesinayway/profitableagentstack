About $106/mo + €38 Waalaxy (2 users) for our recommended stack, estimated from published list prices as of 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-23 (per tool). ## Who this is for

Two to five people share go-to-market. Warm intros and hand outbound already produce some meetings, and you now want a repeatable sequence plus a little content.

### Spreadsheet or CRM?

A shared sheet can work for a short while, but at 2–5 a second person usually already works the pipeline, which is the clearest signal to add a CRM.

Signals that it is time for a CRM (our rules of thumb, not benchmarks):

- **A second person works the pipeline.** A cofounder, first seller, contractor SDR or agency. Shared sheets struggle with ownership, duplicates and overwritten notes.
- **More than about 20–30 live deals or conversations at once.** Or a follow-up has slipped more than once.
- **You copy sequencer replies into the sheet by hand.** More than about 30 minutes a week. Most CRMs here receive replies from Instantly, Smartlead or lemlist directly.
- **Email and LinkedIn reach the same people.** One record per person stops you double-touching prospects.
- **Investor or board pipeline reporting takes more than about an hour a month.** Stage history and pipeline reports are built into every CRM listed, including free plans (depth varies).
- **Inbound leads arrive or your first sales hire starts.** Forms, routing and activity history make the handoff work.

Starter sheet columns: Company, Contact, Email, LinkedIn URL, Source, Stage, Next step, Next-step date, Deal value, Last touch, Notes. Every CRM listed imports a CSV, so nothing is lost when you switch. List prices as of 2026-11-10 from vendor pricing pages; verify with the vendor. Seat counts follow this page's assumptions. - List building & lead data

- LinkedIn outreach
- Search performance
- Social scheduling

- Newsletters
- Web analytics
- Signals, intent & analytics

## Skip for now

- **Paid signals & intent data.** Use at most the free RB2B plan, and only once outbound is booking meetings.
- **AI SDR / agentic SDR.** Contact-sales pricing and little to feed it yet.
- **Content optimization (Surfer), video, community.** Use Search Console and publish first; add paid content tools once content is a weekly habit.
- **AEO (AI search visibility) tools.** Skip until AI assistants send you visits you can see in analytics; warm leads and outbound still produce more pipeline than AI-search visibility.
