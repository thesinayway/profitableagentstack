Can AI agents use this data?

Yes. Fetch /api/tools.json (JSON) or read /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt. All pages are server-rendered HTML with schema.org JSON-LD.

Schema

tools[]: name, use_case, stages[], status (published | contact | unverified), from (lowest published monthly price or null), price (text as published), source (vendor pricing URL), fit_score (editorial 1–5), cost_score, value_score, reviews{g2_rating, g2_review_count, g2_url}. Also use_cases[], stages[], stacks{}, checked (date).
